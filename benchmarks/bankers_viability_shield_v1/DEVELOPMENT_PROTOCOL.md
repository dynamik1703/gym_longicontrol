Do not instantiate BANKERS_DEV_V1 before physical/integrity gates pass. Then select the first 18 route-clean tasks from 11500000 upward using numeric identity and route/full-task fingerprints only. Exclude all inherited ledger reservations and canonical streams, opened panels, internal duplicates and numeric Paper identities 4000–4017. Freeze ordered identities before any performance reset. No deadline-based removal.

At most 54 controller-task episodes: A frozen CAUSAL60 physical reference; B descending native-action preference with the inherited native post-shield predicates and frozen CAUSAL60 replacement; C the identical descending preference restricted to BankerShield's 21-action pre-mask. Descending preference is always 1.0,0.9,...,-1.0, independent of outcomes. B proposes +1 each step; C selects the first admitted choice. B/C execution differs by replacement versus pre-selection; their performance difference is not a pure shield-quality effect.

At every B/C state compare all 21 native proposals under both gates using matched state and causal signs. Report endpoint failures as physically unsafe; failed brake witnesses as uncertifiable, not proven unsafe; deadline-only disagreements separately. The old target-speed cap is not silently applied to the new native menu; the audit distinguishes inherited predicate reuse from its original 60 km/h proposal domain. The historical implementation remains immutable.

All scientific steps retain full native evidence, including masks, certificate digests and selected full certificate, ordinary observations, propagated state, remembered signs/envelope, executed state/action, actual limit, exact excess, signed energy and termination. The inherited deadline diagnostic retains completion_bound_s and applicability whenever computed. Every pair is durably STARTED before reset, executed once under an exclusive writer, independently replayed, atomically written and hashed, then COMPLETED. An ambiguous STARTED pair can never be rerun. Completed pairs may be verified and remaining NOT_STARTED pairs resumed only under identical source/config/split identity.

Limits: active compute <=6h, retained artifacts <=3GiB, no RL updates. Resource projection uses fixture query costs and at most 54*1800 decisions, accounting for matched 21-action comparisons. If limits or integrity gates fail, stop without shrinking the panel. Development is descriptive, never Final Validation or paper evidence.
