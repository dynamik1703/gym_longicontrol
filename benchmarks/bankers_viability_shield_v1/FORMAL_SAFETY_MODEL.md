# Conditional speed-safety model

The information state is the exact action-propagated production VehicleState plus the ordinary normalized 8-vector, remembered observed sign intervals, a pointwise lower envelope of all observation-derived limit constraints, and the observed frontier. There is no route, seed or oracle input. Time, position and signed energy come only from reset and previous executed actions. Reset is exactly the frozen zero state.

W(z) contains all routes consistent with these observations, the frozen stochastic generator, and known physics. Limits are exact members of {20,30,...,100}/3.6; transitions are separated by at least 100 m, so the two sensor slots expose all changes within 150 m. Synthetic fixtures may start with otherwise unsupported first limits and non-grid positions but preserve the minimum, legal limit menu and sensor completeness; this explicitly enlarges the supported model without weakening the physical task.

Each observation supplies a lower envelope through x+150 minus 1e-7 m. A decoded sign position is enclosed in [x+d-1e-7,x+d+1e-7]. In that interval use the smaller adjacent limit. Unknown positions default to 20/3.6. Legal-limit decoding rejects residuals above 1e-10 m/s. Combining evidence means pointwise maximum of valid lower bounds; discard only positions strictly behind the current position. Never discard earlier future constraints. Bounds stay below the actual route for every W(z).

The model assumes deterministic binary64 production execution on the audited runtime, identical action propagation in controller and simulator, the source/power/config hashes, valid original observations, no unmodeled state disturbance, and bounded sensor reconstruction error. No independent observation-noise bound is invented. The 1e-7 m reconstruction enclosure greatly exceeds the few binary64 additions/subtractions and normalization errors below 1004 m (less than 1e-10 m with the stated operators). Limits are decoded against the exact supported menu, not rounded to relax compliance. The physical speed tolerance remains exactly zero; the 1e-6 m/s certificate margin tightens it.

Let B(s) be the exact repeated -1 production trajectory until velocity <= 20/3.6 - 1e-6, followed forever by -1. Define K_speed as information states with compliant current speed and every finite high-speed B endpoint below the causal lower envelope minus the velocity margin. Beyond the finite prefix the universal minimum proves safety. This is an inner approximation of the robust viability kernel, not its maximal characterization.

Admission tests the exact proposed endpoint and the entire B suffix under the **pre-action** lower envelope. Thus sensing/reaction latency and signs crossed inside the step are included. Future observations cannot be used to justify the proposal. Deadline and energy never enter SpeedCertificate.
