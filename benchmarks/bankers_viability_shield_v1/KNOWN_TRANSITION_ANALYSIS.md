# Known signs

Decode legal limits to the exact production speed menu. Every revealed sign
gets a position enclosure, retained in memory. At every proposed and backup
right endpoint evaluate the persistent limit lower envelope, using the smaller
adjacent limit throughout the sign enclosure. This handles steps crossing a
sign and multiple consecutive reductions. Start the backup at the proposed
successor; do not substitute a pre-action braking-distance test. New knowledge
can tighten the envelope upward but never erase old future lower bounds.
