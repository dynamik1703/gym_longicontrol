# Unknown route and latency

The earliest unobserved transition is beyond the PRE-ACTION observed absolute
frontier x+150 minus reconstruction margin. All positions beyond that frontier
receive the exact global minimum 20/3.6 as lower limit. The proposed displacement
consumes preview before braking can begin. The certificate checks that same
frontier for its exact successor and every discrete backup endpoint. Thus it
cannot borrow a fresh 150 m from an observation not yet received.

As new signs appear they add valid lower bounds. Previously observed portions
remain in memory; the envelope combines all of them by pointwise maximum.
Generator spacing is >=100 m so its two sensor slots expose every transition
inside a 150 m window. This completeness assumption is essential; a different
track generator with three arbitrarily close signs needs a different frontier.
The 40 km/h adjacent-change bound is audited but is not needed: using the
universal 20 km/h floor is conservative for every possible later sequence.
