# Research Question

Can a genuinely causal, deterministic and physically richer 100 m MPC improve
energy use over the unchanged `TIME_AWARE` controller without losing hard-task
coverage, and does state-dependent time pricing add executed control authority
beyond one fixed price?

The preregistered mechanisms are: V1's restricted constant-target family (H1),
its compulsory terminal acceleration toward 60 km/h (H2), and weak executed
authority after fallback/shielding (H3). The study may distinguish these
mechanisms; it is not required to produce a positive result.

SlackTrader V1 remains permanently stopped with
`RL_NOT_JUSTIFIED_BY_MPC_PREFLIGHT`. This V2 study does not reinterpret, edit or
replace that finding.
