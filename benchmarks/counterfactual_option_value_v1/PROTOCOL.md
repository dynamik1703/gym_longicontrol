# Prospective Protocol

Source commit is `29b31837984b654e9011d3e180a8e33926345489`.
Temporal Options V1 remains read-only and classified
`SEMANTIC_OR_INTEGRITY_STOP`; none of its 72 Capability pairs may be rerun.

Three route-clean, route-grouped panels are selected performance-blind in
ascending numeric order: 16 Fit routes, 8 Calibration routes and 12 Test routes.
At the first valid strategic boundary at or after 80, 180, 300, 430, 560, 700,
820 and 930 metres, an immutable complete snapshot is taken. From it, one normal
reference and at most two preregistered causal alternatives run to original
termination. Alternative selection never observes counterfactual returns.

`delta_energy_kwh = E_alt - E_ref`; negative values save signed net energy.
Energy labels are valid only for jointly feasible outcomes. Failures are retained
and modeled separately. The privileged best sampled alternative is named
`COUNTERFACTUAL_HINDSIGHT_DIAGNOSTIC` and is not an upper bound or policy.

Fit alone updates a ridge/logistic baseline and three fixed-seed members of a
two-by-64 neural ensemble. Calibration alone chooses the family and a threshold
from the frozen grids. Test predictions, weights, scaling and thresholds are
hashed before Test outcomes are opened. Routes—not snapshots—are the independent
grouping unit.

The prospective opportunity gate is at least 20% of valid Test snapshots with a
genuinely distinguishable feasible alternative saving at least 0.2 Wh. Learning
must reduce Test regret versus always-reference, yield positive net energy after
feasibility filtering, create no hard failure and avoid dependence on only a few
dominant outcomes. Otherwise the corresponding negative or insufficient-evidence
classification is returned.
