# Research Question

Can a predictor using only the frozen 70-dimensional causal observation, causal
option memory, valid actions and action identities identify feasible
energy-saving START/CONTINUE deviations from the non-learning
`CONTINUE_FEEDBACK` heuristic on route-clean unseen tasks?

The pilot distinguishes scarce opportunity, opportunity requiring privileged
future-route knowledge, and opportunity predictable from causal information.
Negative outcomes are retained without post-hoc replacement metrics.
