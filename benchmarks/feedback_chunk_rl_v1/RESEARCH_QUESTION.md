# Research Question

Can a policy that jointly produces three causal one-second corrections to the
frozen AdaptivePrice MPC V2 improve energy and retain all hard requirements,
and does it outperform an otherwise matched one-second residual policy?

The comparison tests temporal coordination, not selection among the historical
17 profiles. A favorable result is not assumed. Feasibility is assessed before
energy, negative results are retained, and no result from Diagnostic may change
the residual range, architecture, reward, exploration, or checkpoint choice.
