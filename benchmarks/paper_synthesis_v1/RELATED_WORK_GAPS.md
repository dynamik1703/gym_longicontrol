# Citation-Needed Register

No external citations are invented in this synthesis. A later literature task
should verify primary sources for the following claims and record exact
bibliographic metadata.

| Topic-specific gap | Question to verify | Intended manuscript use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constrained and safe RL | How do current methods distinguish expected-cost constraints from per-trajectory hard constraints? | Scope the meaning of “hard” requirements and avoid overstating CMDP guarantees. |
| Safety layers and action projection | What guarantees and failure modes are established for shields, safety filters, and control-barrier layers around learned policies? | Position the Hybrid viability mechanism. |
| Residual and hybrid RL control | Under what assumptions have learned residuals improved efficiency without degrading a nominal controller's safety? | Motivate the incremental-learning question and matched baseline. |
| Model-predictive control with learned components | How are learned models, costs, terminal sets, and online adaptation compared with strong non-learning MPC? | Frame FixedPrice/AdaptivePrice and model-based negative findings. |
| Goal-conditioned RL and HER | What evidence exists for sparse-goal success versus satisfaction of continuous trajectory constraints? | Explain why virtual goal relabeling need not certify physical success. |
| Offline supervised goal-conditioned learning | Which distribution-shift and support conditions govern GCSL-style control? | Interpret high completion but low speed compliance without universal claims. |
| Physics-informed RL | Which definitions separate physics-informed observations, physics-shaped objectives, differentiable simulators, and hard physical layers? | Make the method taxonomy precise. |
| Safe exploration and viability | What formal relationships exist among reachability, viability kernels, recursive feasibility, and online shields? | Calibrate claims about the causal physical controller. |
| Benchmark task feasibility | How do control/RL benchmarks screen impossible generated requirements while retaining unbiased evaluation? | Contextualize the retrospective feasibility audit and raw-denominator rule. |
| Reliability statistics | What confidence methods are recommended for paired binary outcomes, few task clusters, and repeated trained seeds? | Validate the planned exact tests and task-cluster bootstrap. |
| Energy-efficient longitudinal control | Which energy models and traffic-free eco-driving baselines are standard, and which comparisons require terminal-state matching? | Bound the interpretation of signed energy. |
| Negative results and preregistration | What reporting standards exist for stopped studies, amended validation, and exploratory post-hoc analysis in ML/control? | Support the evidence hierarchy and incident reporting. |
| Exactly-once scientific evaluation | Which workflow/provenance standards address durable attempt ledgers, atomic artifacts, and resumable simulations? | Ground the final Paper integrity protocol. |

Literature verification must not alter the frozen experimental classifications.
It should distinguish peer-reviewed sources, standards, and preprints and avoid
using survey statements where an original technical result is available.
