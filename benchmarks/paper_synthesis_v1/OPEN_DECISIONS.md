# Open Decisions Requiring Human Authorization

No item below may be resolved by opening Paper content or starting an
experiment in this task.

1. **Primary arms.** Approve or revise the proposed seven-arm set: CAUSAL60,
   TIME_AWARE, FixedPrice, AdaptivePrice, and all three Hybrid seeds.
2. **Constrained V2 provenance.** Decide whether locally present checkpoint
   bytes, whose hashes were audited now but were not embedded in the original
   result record, are admissible as a conditional secondary arm.
3. **Contextual arms.** Decide whether the privileged full-route reference and
   at most one weak learned comparator justify their additional Paper pairs.
4. **Reliability decision rule.** Approve the conservative reporting rule that
   any Hybrid-only failure versus AdaptivePrice blocks an unqualified
   “reliability preserved” claim; decide whether a formal non-inferiority margin
   is scientifically defensible before outcomes exist.
5. **Inference scope.** Approve fixed-panel descriptive intervals and exact
   paired tests, or specify a population-sampling model that would justify
   broader inference. Do not choose this after viewing outcomes.
6. **Multiplicity family.** Confirm that the three seed-specific
   Hybrid-versus-Adaptive reliability and exploratory energy contrasts use Holm
   adjustment while the seed-averaged Hybrid contrast is the single primary
   learning estimand.
7. **Feasibility analysis timing.** Decide whether the authorized future
   workflow performs the frozen mathematical classification after all raw
   outcomes are sealed, as drafted, or in an independently owned preregistered
   pre-analysis. Either choice must preserve raw RSR and prevent task-informed
   controller changes.
8. **Execution implementation.** Authorize a separate task to implement and
   test exactly-once ledgers, durable full-state resume, atomic results,
   `completion_bound_s` retention, and independent verification. This draft
   intentionally supplies no Paper runner.
9. **Execution ownership.** Name the exclusive process/operator, incident
   authority, storage location, and permanent sealing procedure.
10. **Literature verification.** Authorize a separate external literature
    search for `RELATED_WORK_GAPS.md` before manuscript claims are cited.
11. **Final Paper opening.** Provide explicit written authorization only after
    arms, hashes, code, statistics, and safeguards are frozen. The present
    synthesis is not that authorization.
