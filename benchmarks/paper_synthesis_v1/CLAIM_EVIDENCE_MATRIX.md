## Claim boundary

The proposed paper thesis is therefore deliberately asymmetric: there is
direct evidence that explicit constraints and physical structure mattered on
specific matched panels, but the incremental benefit of learning over the best
matched physical optimizer is not yet established. Cross-panel counts remain
context, not a leaderboard.
