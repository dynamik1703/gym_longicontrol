# Figure and Table Plan

All plotted values must be generated from the JSON/CSV registries or referenced
immutable records. Route repetitions across controllers are paired marks, not
new independent routes.

## Figures

1. **Evidence architecture.** Flow from reward-based learning through explicit
   constraints and physical references to Hybrid and improved MPC, annotated
   with which contrasts are paired and which are contextual.
2. **Requirement decomposition.** Completion, deadline, speed, and joint RSR
   for the Same-Information comparison, GCSL, and Constrained V2. Facet by
   study; do not align bars as a common-population ranking.
3. **Paired Hybrid energy effects.** Per-task percentage savings for H11, H29,
   and H47 versus TIME_AWARE on 35 joint successes, with task-connected marks,
   arithmetic means, medians, ranges, and descriptive task-bootstrap intervals.
4. **MPC attribution.** Paired task distributions for FixedPrice versus
   TIME_AWARE and AdaptivePrice versus FixedPrice on the same 18 capability
   tasks; label the changed design factors.
5. **Feasibility proof schematic.** Two speed-limit segments, possible boundary
   steps, interior distance, and conservative travel-time lower bound for task
   10200029.
6. **Historical feasibility audit.** Witness/proof/unresolved counts for all
   411 unique tasks and the accepted-panel subset, without interpreting these
   convenience samples as prevalence estimates.

## Tables

1. **Method contract registry.** Condensed method family, actor information,
   physics mechanism, training budget, evaluation panel, RSR, and frozen
   classification; machine source `method_registry.json`.
2. **Evidence and comparability.** Every headline comparison with basis,
   distinct routes, controller-task pairs, and qualification; machine source
   `master_evidence_matrix.csv`.
3. **Matched learning results.** Same-Information, constrained, goal/future,
   and model-based results, grouped by study rather than globally ranked.
4. **Physical and hybrid results.** Physical reference, Hybrid V2, and causal
   MPC panels with explicit panel boundaries and information contracts.
5. **Negative/stopped studies.** Trigger, completed work, missing endpoint, and
   classification for learned MBRL, SlackTrader, Temporal Options, and
   Counterfactual Option Value.
6. **Feasibility audit.** Proven-infeasible task identities, acceptance status,
   continuous bound, conservative discrete bound, and margin.
7. **Final Paper protocol.** Predeclared arms, checkpoint hashes, endpoints,
   contrasts, and contextual status; include only after human authorization.
