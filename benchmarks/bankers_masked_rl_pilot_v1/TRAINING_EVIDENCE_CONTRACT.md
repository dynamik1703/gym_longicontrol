# bankers-training-evidence-v1 component contract

The implemented row contains Attempt/source identity; canonical task/cursor;
full causal memory and observation; current and successor physical masks and
policy supports; exact current/successor state bindings; proposal/executed grid
IDs and replacement reason; complete selected production certificate/digest;
reward and separate costs; actual successor state, signed energy and positive
speed excess; terminal/TimeLimit/budget-cutoff flags; and replay insertion ID.

Hypothetical candidate trajectories are not retained in ordinary training rows.
They remain reproducible from frozen physical sources and causal states. Full
selected-action online certification remains mandatory on every Banker action.
The proposed independent training audit checks every executed action and actual
transition and samples both complete current and successor grids at native
insertion IDs divisible by 100. This would be a sampled grid audit, never an
exhaustive training-mask audit. Diagnostic evidence would retain and independently
check every grid to V4 semantics.

The independent verifier currently fails before its first native evidence row
is accepted. Durable scientific journal publication, independent end-to-end
auditing and sealing were not completed or resource measured. There are no
scientific training evidence journals to verify in this stopped package.
