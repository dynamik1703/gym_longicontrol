# Causal information boundary

The component policy input is identical across arms: the original eight-vector
plus position and elapsed time propagated from executed actions. PRE receives
its deterministic causal certified support. Physical memory retains only
causally observed signs and the unchanged conservative envelope/frontier.

No route seed, hidden future route, oracle trajectory, future outcome or held-out
content enters the actor/critic input. Training task identity and actual route
are audit-only metadata. Environment state and track may be inspected by an
independent verifier, never by policy inference. No historical Validation or
Paper environment was reset in this pilot.

The interface has not passed the complete end-to-end preflight and is not a
released scientific learner interface.
