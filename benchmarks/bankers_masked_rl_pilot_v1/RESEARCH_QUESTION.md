# Research question

Does physical native-action pre-masking improve reliable task learning relative
to identical unrestricted or post-corrected discrete SAC? The matched pilot
was to use seed 11, 50,000 transitions and 5,000 updates per arm, followed by
12 fresh Policy-R tasks and three fixed physical reference controllers.

The question remains unanswered: independent evidence verification failed
before scientific training. No performance, safety during learned execution,
generalization or method-superiority conclusion can be drawn.
